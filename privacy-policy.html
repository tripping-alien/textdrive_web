Information We Collect & How We Use It

Depending on the specific game and user interaction, information is processed across two distinct tiers:

A. Locally Stored Device Data (On-Device Only)

Our games store state information locally on your Android device using Unity's PlayerPrefs and local file sandboxes:

  • Gameplay Progress: Highest scores, wave completions, unlocked levels, unlocked skins, and virtual currency tallies.
  • Configuration Preferences: Sound effects volume, music volume, vibration toggles, and screen orientation preferences.
  • Rating Prompts: Local counters to prevent repetitive review popups (e.g. SmartRateUs counter).

Crucial Note: This data remains isolated within your device's sandbox. It is never transmitted to, backed up on, or inspected by our private web servers.

B. Automated Technical & Device Data (Third-Party Partners)

To maintain 60 FPS performance, prevent crashes, and deliver digital ads in free editions, our games may integrate sanctioned industry-standard SDKs (Unity, Google AdMob, Google Firebase). These third parties automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical telemetry as outlined in sections 4, 5, and 6.

4

Unity Technologies & Game Engine Runtime

Our games are constructed using the Unity Engine (developed by Unity Software Inc. / Unity Technologies). As part of the core runtime engine operation, Unity may automatically collect anonymous hardware and runtime data.

  • Unique Device Identifiers: Randomly generated installation GUIDs or platform identifiers (IDFV on iOS, Android ID on Android).
  • Hardware Specifications: Device model, CPU architecture, GPU chipset, RAM size, screen resolution, and operating system build version.
  • Coarse Location: IP address used strictly to deduce country/region for localization and regional language delivery (not precise GPS location).
  • Engine Diagnostics: Rendering frame rates, graphics API compatibility (Vulkan, OpenGLES 3.0), and low-level engine exceptions.

For comprehensive details on how Unity processes runtime data, please review the official Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising Network)

To keep our games free to download and play for everyone around the world, free editions may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Ad Format When Displayed Data Processed by Google
Banner Ads Non-intrusively anchored at the screen bottom or top. Google Advertising ID (AAID/GAID), IP address, device model.
Interstitial Ads Natural breaks between gameplay sessions (e.g. Game Over). Ad impression data, click metrics, time spent viewing.
Rewarded Videos Voluntarily chosen by user to gain extra lives or dice. Ad completion events, reward authorization token.

Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

  • Personalized Ads: Delivered based on interests inferred from apps you use and websites you visit, using your device's Google Advertising ID. In regions requiring explicit consent (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR and California under CPRA), personalized ads are only served after obtaining user consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Non-Personalized Ads: If consent is declined or if you have opted out of ad tracking, advertisements are contextual—based solely on general game context, coarse location (country), and current device type.

How to Opt Out of Interest-Based Advertising:

You have full control over personalized ads across all your Android apps:

  • On Your Device: Open Android Settings > Google > Ads > Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Via Google Account: Visit Google Ads Settings to manage personalization preferences.
  • Learn more in the Google Advertising & Privacy Guidelines.

Google Firebase (Crash Diagnostics & Analytics)

We may employ Google Firebase (Google LLC) services to safeguard app performance, stability, and bug resolution:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: When an unexpected game crash occurs, Crashlytics records an anonymous crash dump: stack trace, device state (battery percentage, free disk space, orientation), and device model. This data contains zero personal information and is used exclusively by our engineering team to publish bug-fix releases.
  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregates high-level statistical events (such as "Level 5 Completed" or "Game Started") to evaluate which gameplay mechanics are enjoyable and where balance adjustments are required.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: Tracks launch latency and screen rendering speed to guarantee smooth 60 FPS gameplay across diverse Android chipsets.

Firebase data is stored securely in Google Cloud data centers with strict encryption at rest and in transit. Read the Google Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

In-App Purchases & Financial Data

Certain SmallTalk titles offer optional In-App Purchases (IAP) (e.g. "Remove Ads" or cosmetic upgrades):

  • Handled Exclusively by Google Play: All financial transactions, credit card processing, billing address verification, and currency conversions are handled 100% by the Google Play In-App Billing API.
  • No Financial Data Stored: SmallTalk Apps never receives, views, or stores your credit card numbers, PayPal credentials, or banking details.
  • Fulfillment Verification: Google Play communicates solely an encrypted receipt token to our app confirming whether a product ID was purchased, which unlocks the corresponding premium feature locally on your device.

Android System Permissions Explained

We strictly practice Permission Minimization. Our games request only the minimum Android OS permissions required for basic operation:

Permission Category Functional Reason
INTERNET Normal Required to retrieve advertisement banners, submit anonymous crash reports, and open developer links if tapped.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Checks whether WiFi or mobile cellular data is active before initiating network attempts.
VIBRATE Normal Provides tactile haptic response on physical collisions, button taps, and game actions (can be muted in-game).
BILLING Normal Connects to Google Play In-App Billing for optional premium ad removal purchases.
🔒 No Dangerous Permissions

Our games do NOT request runtime dangerous permissions: no GPS location access, no microphone access, no camera access, and no address book contacts access.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Protecting young players is paramount to SmallTalk Apps. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

European Economic Area & UK Rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, your personal data is protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Legal Bases for Processing

  • User Consent: We request your explicit consent for personalized advertisements and analytics cookies via an interactive Google-certified consent banner on initial launch. You can modify or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: Necessary processing for app security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, and ensuring smooth 60 FPS gameplay.

Your Statutory GDPR Rights

  • Right to Access: Request confirmation on whether personal data relating to you is being processed.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your technical data or Advertising ID.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Object: Object to processing of your data for advertising purposes.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive machine-readable copies of any provided data.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the legal right to file a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (DPA).

Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Security Safeguards

All network communications initiated by our applications and integrated SDKs employ strong encryption standards (Transport Layer Security / HTTPS).

Data Retention

Local game progress persists on your device until uninstalled or manually cleared. Diagnostic telemetry gathered by Google Firebase and AdMob is retained according to Google's standard retention cycles (typically 2 to 14 months before automated aggregation and deletion).

How to Completely Delete All Stored Game Data:

You have 100% control over locally stored data. To wipe all progress, scores, and cached preferences:

  • Open your Android device Settings.
  • Navigate to Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  • Select the specific SmallTalk Game (e.g. Physics Sandbox 2D or Car Dodge).
  • Tap Storage & Cache.
  • Select Clear Storage (or Clear Data). All local data is instantly purged.
  • Uninstalling the game permanently erases all associated sandbox storage.
